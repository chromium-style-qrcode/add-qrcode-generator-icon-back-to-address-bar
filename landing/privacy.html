Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-19

Add QR Code Generator Icon Back To Address Bar is a Chrome extension. Short version: it generates QR codes entirely on your device. It does not collect your personal information and does not upload your data to us.

What it stores

Your settings are saved locally using chrome.storage. That covers your QR code appearance preferences (logo, module shape, corner style, colors) and your generation history. If you activate Pro, your license key and activation status are also stored locally.

If you have Chrome sync turned on, these settings may sync across your own devices through Google's sync system. They never leave that system, and they are never sent to us.

How QR generation works

QR codes are generated locally on your device. The text or URL you encode — and the images you decode — never leave your browser. Decoding uses your browser's built-in detector or a local library; uploaded images are never transmitted anywhere.

Third-party sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising, analytics, or any purpose unrelated to operating the extension and fulfilling your Pro purchase. Your data is disclosed to third parties only in the limited cases below, where each party is a distinct service provider receiving only the data it needs:

  • Google Chrome sync — if you have browser sync enabled, your settings and generation history are synced across your own devices through Google's sync system. This is optional and controlled entirely by your Chrome settings.
  • Waffo Pancake (payment processor) — when you purchase Pro, Waffo Pancake acts as the merchant of record and processes the payment. We receive from them your checkout email address and an order reference so we can issue your license key. See "Payment information" below.
  • Our license verification server (license.add-qr-code-generator-icon-back-to-address-bar.dev) — receives only the license key and a random device identifier when you activate or validate Pro, to bind the license to your device.

Payment information

Pro purchases are processed entirely by Waffo Pancake, which acts as the merchant of record. The payment form, card details, and billing information are collected and handled by Waffo Pancake — we never see or store your card number, expiry, or CVV, and those details never pass through our extension or servers.

After a completed purchase, we retain only the following purchase-related data to issue, verify, and support your license:

  • the email address you entered at checkout;
  • the order reference assigned by Waffo Pancake;
  • the license key we issued to you;
  • the device identifiers bound to that license (up to the activation limit).

This record is kept for as long as your license is active, and for a reasonable period afterwards so we can restore or verify it if you reinstall. You can request deletion of this record at any time — see "Your data rights" below.

Permissions

storage — saves the settings and history described above.

activeTab — granted only when you click the icon or use a context menu, so the extension can read the current page's URL to pre-fill the QR code.

contextMenus — adds "Create QR code" items to your right-click menu.

What it does not do

  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads.
  • No collecting personal info, browsing history, or page content.
  • No uploading the text you encode or the images you decode.

License activation

When you activate a Pro license, the extension sends the license key and a random device identifier to our license server (license.add-qr-code-generator-icon-back-to-address-bar.dev) to verify the key and bind it to your device. The server stores the license key, the email you used at checkout, the order reference from our payment processor, and the device identifiers bound to the key. No other data is transmitted.

Your data rights

You can review, correct, and delete everything the extension stores, without contacting us:

  • Review and change — open the extension options to see and change your settings, clear your history, and activate or deactivate your license.
  • Delete everything — either click "Reset to defaults" in the extension options, clear the extension's site data in chrome://extensions, or uninstall the extension, which removes all of its stored data.

For the license record held on our server, you can request access, correction, or deletion by emailing liuliang@webfrontend.dev from the email you used at purchase. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
