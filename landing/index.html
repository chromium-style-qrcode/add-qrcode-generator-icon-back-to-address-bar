Why

Chrome used to show a QR code for the current page right in the address bar, but the feature was removed. This extension brings it back — with the exact same visual style: round modules, rounded finder patterns, and the Chrome dinosaur in the center.

Everything runs on your device. Your URLs and text never leave your browser.

Questions

Is it really the same as Chrome's native QR code?

Yes — it uses the same generation algorithm and the same visual style (round modules, rounded finder patterns, the Chrome dinosaur center). That's the whole point of the extension.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. QR codes are generated entirely on your device. No analytics, no tracking, no accounts.

What does Pro unlock?

SVG export, high-resolution PNG, batch generation (ZIP), vCard/calendar QR codes, and a 500-item history. It's a one-time $9.99 purchase — no subscription, no auto-renewal.

What permissions does it need?

Three: storage (save settings and history), activeTab (read the current page URL when you click the icon), and contextMenus (the right-click "Create QR code" items).
